# Advertising domain data

`Orvlet/Resources/adblock-rules.json` includes a domain-only snapshot of Peter Lowe’s ad and tracking server list, retrieved on 2026-11-01 and transformed into WebKit content-blocking rules. The list is maintained by Peter Lowe, https://pgl.yoyo.org/as/ . Its published permission allows combining and republishing the list (see the “submit a hostname” section of that page). Orvlet-specific cosmetic and YouTube endpoint rules are separate additions.

Source: https://pgl.yoyo.org/adservers/serverlist.php?hostformat=nohtml&showintro=0&mimetype=plaintext

Domain count: 3561

These rules run only in Orvlet’s built-in browser. They do not alter system DNS, hosts files, external browsers, or proxy settings. This is a bundled snapshot, not a claim of automatic updates or universal ad removal.
