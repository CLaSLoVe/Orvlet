A calm agent for your Mac

Your work. Still in focus.

Delegate a task. Keep doing your thing.
Return when you need to check or adjust it.

Designed for intermittent attention

An ongoing task.
A moment of attention.

Calm Agent connects background execution with brief, purposeful interaction. A task keeps its goal and context while you focus on primary work. When you return, its current state and useful controls are within reach.

Read the concept ↗
Delegate
Express or revise a goal in language. “Play some quiet music.” Later, “Switch to instrumental jazz.”
Adjust
Use a familiar control for a precise change. Pause playback or change a timer value.
Check
Glance at the current state. See what is playing, what remains, or whether the task needs your input.
About the film

A researcher starts a writing session, asks Orvlet for lofi music, and continues working while a compact player appears. A meeting notice becomes a calendar task; Orvlet asks for the missing start time and confirms the saved event. The researcher returns to writing, then asks for a different soundtrack.

The film uses real prototype captures within a staged work scenario. Manuscript and messages are fictional; some waiting periods are shortened. Music is an editorial replacement soundtrack. There is no spoken narration; the story is told through on-screen text and interaction.

Orvlet illustrates the framework in Calm Agent: Designing for Intermittent Human Attention, by Dian Jin and Yancheng Yuan.
