Film notes & credits

Calm Agent uses real prototype captures in a staged work scenario. Calm Workday adds illustrative interface sequences. Some waiting periods are shortened; music is an editorial soundtrack.

Calm Workday music: Cloud (lofi and jazz hiphop versions) — original music by clas233. Calm Agent music and shared sound effects:

  • Full Chill Lofi Beat.wav — CrushedLoops
  • Circuit Synthwave Alt — Doctor_Dreamchip
  • Typing on a computer keyboard — Vtrmrll
  • Mouse 2 Button Fast Click — aphom000

All four recordings are listed as CC0 1.0 in the production credits. Audio is trimmed, mixed, and faded for the film; the lofi track is looped.
